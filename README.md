# 📊 Week 2 - Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA) & Visualization

This repository contains the deliverables for **Week 2 of the NextGen Learners Data Science Internship 2026**.  
The focus of this task was **exploring the cleaned applicant dataset, finding patterns, and presenting them with clear visualizations**.



## 🎯 Task Objectives
- Perform **Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA)** on the cleaned dataset.  
- Answer 4 required questions + 1 bonus question with **aggregation + visualization + takeaway**.  
- Communicate insights clearly for a non‑technical audience.  
- Summarize findings in a **Key Insights** section.



## 🛠 Tools & Libraries
- **Python 3.x**  
- **Pandas** (aggregation, pivot tables)  
- **Matplotlib & Seaborn** (visualizations)  



## 📊 Analysis Performed
1. **Domain Popularity** → Bar chart showing number of applications per domain.  
   - *Insight:* Graphic Design received the highest applications, followed by Digital Marketting.  

2. **Applications Trend Over Time** → Line chart grouped by month.  
   - *Insight:* Applications peaked in August 2026, stayed strong in September, and declined in October.  

3. **Acceptance Rate (Overall + Per Domain)** → Pie chart + grouped bar chart.  
   - *Insight:* Overall acceptance rate was 22%. Graphic Design showed relatively higher success compared to Digital Marketting.  

4. **Top Universities** → Horizontal bar chart of top 10 universities.  
   - *Insight:* Punjab University contributed the highest applicants, followed by The University of Faisalabad and COMSATS University.  

5. **Bonus Chart (Applications by Day of Week)** → Bar chart.  
   - *Insight:* Most applications were submitted on Sundays and Mondays.  



## ✅ Key Insights
- Graphic Design is the most popular domain, but acceptance rates vary across domains.  
- Applications peaked in August 2026, then declined toward October.  
- Punjab University, The University of Faisalabad, and COMSATS University dominate applicant sources.  
- Overall acceptance rate is 22%, with Graphic Design showing stronger relative success.  
- Applications are concentrated on Sundays and Mondays.
